CLIPFORGE – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

CLIPFORGE ("the app") is a desktop application, operated by [Rei], that runs entirely on the user's own computer. This policy explains what data it accesses and how that data is handled.

1. Data CLIPFORGE accesses

When you connect a platform account, and only with your permission, CLIPFORGE accesses:

  • TikTok: your basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) and creator posting information. It uses the permission to upload and publish videos that you choose.
  • YouTube: your channel name and ID. It uses the permission to upload videos that you choose.
  • Instagram: your username, account type and ID. It uses the permission to publish Reels that you choose.
  • Twitch: public stream information, and your username and permission to create clips if you connect your Twitch account.

2. How the data is used

This data is used only to display your connected accounts inside the app and to publish the videos you select to those accounts. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

3. Where the data is stored

  • All data stays on your own computer. CLIPFORGE has no servers and does not collect, sell or share your data with anyone.
  • Access tokens are stored encrypted, protected by the Windows Credential Manager.
  • Video files are sent only to the platform you publish to. For Instagram, a video may be placed temporarily on a storage service you configure, and it is deleted automatically after publishing.

4. Third parties

Data is exchanged only with the platforms you connect (TikTok, Google/YouTube, Meta/Instagram, Twitch), under their own privacy policies.

5. Deleting your data

You can disconnect any account in CLIPFORGE (Social Accounts → Disconnect), which deletes its stored tokens. You can also revoke access in the platform's own settings. Uninstalling CLIPFORGE and deleting the folder %APPDATA%\CLIPFORGE removes all remaining data.

6. Children

CLIPFORGE is not intended for anyone under 13.
